# InkRelay for Mac

Handwritten notes from any e-ink tablet, delivered into the notes apps you already use.

**[Download InkRelay for Mac](https://github.com/urazalievf/inkrelay-releases/releases/latest/download/InkRelay.zip)** · [all versions and what changed](https://github.com/urazalievf/inkrelay-releases/releases)

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Install

1. Open `InkRelay.zip`; it unpacks to `InkRelay.app`.
2. Move `InkRelay.app` to Applications and open it. It lives in the menu bar.
3. Follow the first-run steps to connect your tablet and choose where notes go.

InkRelay looks here once a day for a newer version and tells you when there is one. Nothing about your Mac is sent.

## Free and Pro

Free for folder exports, Obsidian and the Apple apps. Pro ($29 once, with a 14-day trial) adds the cloud sources, Google, Microsoft and Notion, AI transcription and the review stack. No subscription: a Pro key is yours.
